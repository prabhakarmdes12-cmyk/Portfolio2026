From Opaque Chemical Invoices to Real-Time GC/MS Transparency: Engineering a Technical-Premium B2B Procurement Experience.

TS Aromatics is a specialized B2B commerce platform for therapeutic-grade essential oils and botanical isolates. The legacy trading ecosystem relied on opaque paper certificates, slow email negotiations, and delayed laboratory verifications. We architected a unified digital platform featuring real-time GC/MS purity visualizers, tiered bulk ordering, and an interactive learning academy.

Role Lead Product Designer
Timeline 2026 · Shipped to Production
Context TS Aromatics · B2B Chemical & Flavoring Commerce

My Role & Contributions
Focus Area What I Designed & Delivered
Information Architecture Restructured 400+ botanical SKUs with chemical constituents, origins, CAS numbers, and certifications.
GC/MS Data Visualizer Designed interactive chromatographic spectrum viewer allowing buyers to inspect purity batches before ordering.
Tiered Bulk Quoting Streamlined tiered volume pricing, custom packaging requests, and sample kit ordering.
Gamified Academy Engineered customer onboarding certification modules to train industrial flavorists and formulators.

Overview & The Procurement Challenge

Wholesale procurement in the essential oils and aroma chemicals industry has traditionally been characterized by severe friction. Buyers—ranging from cosmetics formulators to pharmaceutical manufacturers—require verifiable batch testing (GC/MS, optical rotation, refractive index) before issuing multimillion-rupee purchase orders.

Legacy suppliers treated this data as an afterthought, emailing unsearchable scanned PDFs days after a quote request. Our objective was to make technical transparency TS Aromatics' primary competitive moat.

The "Technical Premium" UX Philosophy

We balanced rigorous chemical data density with high-end luxury aesthetics. Every product page acts as a digital technical datasheet, providing interactive chromatography peaks alongside sensory aroma wheels, botanical family trees, and harvest origins.

Core Interaction Pillars

  • Interactive GC/MS Spectrum Viewer: Zoom into retention times, view constituent breakdown percentages, and verify natural versus synthetic markers.
  • Instant Batch CoA Download: One-click verified certificate retrieval linked to QR codes on physical drum packaging.
  • Dynamic Volume Tier Pricing: Live price-per-kg calculations based on drum volume, freight selection, and export tariffs.

Education & Gamified Academy

To turn buyers into long-term partners, we embedded the TS Academy directly into the procurement workflow. Young perfumers and extraction technicians complete interactive modules on terpene profiles, carrier compatibility, and safety standards, unlocking preferential sample credits upon completion.

Translating 30 Years of Private High-Jewelry Heritage into a Digital Salon: The Obsidian Gilt Experience.

House of Giriraj (Shree Giriraj Jewellers) has handcrafted bespoke Polki, Jadau, and Colombian Emerald masterworks for royalty and discerning collectors across three decades. Moving from invitation-only physical salons to an international digital flagship required a design philosophy of 'Slow Luxury'—prioritizing tactile storytelling, microscopic gem provenance, and private concierge conversion over generic transactional checkout carts.

Overview & The B2B-to-D2C Challenge

High jewelry is fundamentally an intimate, sensory purchase. Discerning clientele buying unheated Zambian emeralds or uncut Polki diamonds expect discretion, bespoke consultations, and heirloom-level storytelling. Standard eCommerce cart buttons feel tacky and diminish the perceived worth of multi-million rupee masterworks.

Our mandate was to build a digital salon that inspires the same awe and reverence as stepping into an exclusive private jewelry atelier in Jaipur or Geneva.

Obsidian Gilt — Slow Luxury Design System

We formulated the Obsidian Gilt aesthetic:

  • Basalt Obsidian Background: Deep #09090b charcoal that allows natural gemstone color saturation and fire dispersion to pop without glare.
  • Brushed Gilt Accents: Warm champagne gold borders and delicate serif typography inspired by vintage jewelry auction catalogs.
  • Tactile Micro-Interactions: Fluid 60fps parallax transitions that emulate holding a fine jewelry piece up to the light.

Curated High-Jewelry Showcase

Each piece in the collection is presented as an individual work of art, accompanied by its metal purity, gem carat weight, artisan provenance, and certificate authentication:

WhatsApp Smart Enquiry Funnel

Instead of impersonal forms, each piece features a 'Private Client Consultation' trigger that opens WhatsApp with a pre-configured, personalized payload containing the exact SKU name, carat specifications, and direct link back to the private gallery view.

Gamifying Nutrition & Joyful Habits for Rural Tweens: Zero-Anxiety Health Architecture in Jharkhand.

Orry is an empathetic pediatric wellness and nutrition platform designed specifically for tweens (ages 9–14) across rural and semi-urban schools in Jharkhand. Tackling adolescent malnutrition and sedentary screen fatigue required discarding clinical charts and adult medical jargon. We co-created a culturally rooted, mascot-driven experience that transforms daily healthy habits into joyful micro-missions—supported by a lightweight supervisor dashboard for teachers and healthcare workers.

Overview & Problem: The Tween Dilemma

Adolescent nutrition in rural India faces twin challenges: micronutrient deficiencies (iron-deficiency anemia, stunting) combined with a sudden surge in packaged ultra-processed junk food consumption. Adult health apps full of calorie counting, BMI tables, and shame-inducing streak counters cause severe anxiety and are completely ineffective for 10-year-olds.

Core Design Philosophy: The 3 Pillars

Branding, Mascot & Visual Language

The visual identity was designed in collaboration with students. Orry's shape is friendly, dynamic, and non-judgmental. We utilized vibrant local colors—sunflower yellow, clay terracotta, and leafy mint—paired with bold, readable typography that supports both Hindi and English.

The Student Experience: Frictionless Habit Loops

Check-ins take less than 30 seconds. Children tap what they ate from colorful local food cards (dal, ragi, seasonal fruit, clean water) or log outdoor playground games. If a student misses three days, Orry greets them with warmth rather than a broken streak badge.

Bridging Server Room Diagnostics with Executive Realities: Enterprise Infrastructure Command at Scale.

NetQ Command is an enterprise-scale network infrastructure observability and telemetry platform designed for Fortune 500 Network Operations Centers (NOC). Managing thousands of switches, cloud fabrics, and telemetry streams often results in alert fatigue and delayed incident triage. We architected a dual-viewport interface that simultaneously provides low-level network packet telemetry to DevOps engineers and high-level SLA/business impact monitoring to executive leadership.

Role Lead Product Designer
Timeline 2025 · Enterprise Deployment
Context NetQ Systems · High-Availability Observability SaaS
Architecture Micro-Frontend Architecture, Real-Time WebSocket Canvas
Enterprise SaaS Network Observability Real-Time Data Incident Triage UX Design Systems
My Role & Contributions
Focus Area What I Designed & Delivered
Dual-Audience Architecture Created dual-layer viewports: NOC Engineer Granular Terminal vs Executive SLA Impact Canvas.
Topological Visualizer Designed interactive node-graph visualizer representing multi-cloud hybrid network topologies.
Intelligent Deduplication Designed machine-learning alert clustering UX reducing noise by 84% during cascading outages.
Automated Playbooks Engineered single-click remediation workflows for recurring infrastructure incidents.

The Enterprise Infrastructure Problem

When a primary data center switch fails, it triggers thousands of downstream error alerts across servers, load balancers, databases, and microservices within seconds. Network engineers are flooded with red sirens, while executive leadership has no immediate way of knowing which revenue-generating customer applications are actually down.

NetQ Command solves this by correlating raw physical layer telemetry directly with business SLA dependencies.

Dual-Viewport Architecture

The system provides two synchronized perspectives:

  • NOC Terminal View: Real-time packet throughput, BGP peering status, interface error rates, and terminal command execution.
  • Executive SLA Canvas: Revenue at risk, impacted enterprise customers, contracted uptime compliance, and incident resolution timeline.

Interactive Topology Canvas

We engineered a WebGL-powered interactive topology graph capable of rendering 10,000+ nodes with sub-second health transitions. Engineers can pinch-zoom from global multi-cloud regions down to an individual switch port.

Designing India's Vedic Intelligence System of Record: Bridging Ancient Sacred Shastras with Contemporary Mobile Clarity.

Kashi Sahayak V3 represents a zero-to-one design architecture for sacred Hindu sciences. Rather than relying on non-deterministic, hallucinatory generative AI, we architected a 100% deterministic, rule-based computational engine that renders Jyotish calculations, live temple darshan feeds, and Indic astrological consultations with absolute spiritual reverence and low-latency mobile speed.

Role Lead Product Designer & Design Architect
Timeline Oct 2025 – Present · Live 2026
Context Cosmic Tantra · Direct-to-Consumer Vedic AI
Architecture Zero-LLM Deterministic Rules Engine + Indic Typography
0→1 Product Design Deterministic AI UX Indic Design Systems Information Architecture Mobile App Micro-Interactions
My Role & Contributions
Focus Area What I Designed & Delivered
Core UX Architecture Architected zero-LLM conversational state machine and multi-tier Vedic computation flows with sub-second feedback.
Design System ("Kashi") Created dual-state "Subah-e-Banaras" (day) and "Kashi Sandhya" (night) sacred palette and typography system.
Interactive Kundli Engine Designed progressive, collapsible 12-house Vedic birth chart visualization with real-time planetary transits.
Multi-lingual Indic UX Engineered vernacular typography and devotional voice/text input optimized for non-English native users.

Overview & Problem Framing

Modern AI models in the spiritual domain suffer from a fatal flaw: hallucination. While an error in casual creative writing is harmless, an inaccurate horoscope calculation, improper puja tithi, or fabricated Sanskrit sloka shatters user faith instantly.

Our challenge was to build an interface that feels genuinely sacred, deeply reverent, and culturally authentic to Indian users—while remaining 100% deterministic, mathematically accurate, and blazingly fast on constrained mobile connections.

Pillar 1: "Shraddha" Meets "Vigyan"

We rejected standard SaaS minimalism and gaudy commercial astrology patterns. Instead, we established the Kashi Design System rooted in Banaras ritual aesthetics:

SUBAH-E-BANARAS (Day Mode)

Warm sandal parchment, brass morning sunlight, terracotta accents, and high-contrast deep ink typography for outdoor morning puja readings.

KASHI SANDHYA (Night Mode)

Ghat-stone basalt, deep flame amber glowing outlines, and starlight celestial overlays for midnight meditation and quiet introspection.

Pillar 2: Kashi Sahayak V3 Conversational Engine

Kashi Sahayak is not an unconstrained chatbot. It is a strictly guided state-machine conversational interface. Questions are categorized into verified Vedic nodes (Dasha analysis, Panchang timings, Dosha remedies, and Muhurat planning).

Responses cite verified Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra rules, display the planetary mathematical inputs transparently, and never speculate on life expectancy or catastrophic medical outcomes.

Pillar 3: Progressive Kundli & Panchang Experience

Traditional birth charts are notoriously difficult to read on 6-inch mobile screens. We engineered an interactive North & South Indian Kundli renderer that allows users to tap individual houses to inspect planetary aspects, degrees, nakshatras, and friendly/enemy dignities dynamically.
