Overview & The Procurement Challenge

TS Aromatics needed a procurement platform that could communicate technical legitimacy to serious B2B buyers — manufacturers, wellness founders, and formulators who demand GC/MS documentation, molecular transparency, and ingredient intelligence.

The Core Challenge: Competitors in the essential oils space either had deep documentation with poor user experience, or beautiful design with no technical substance. Serious B2B buyers required scientific proof before committing to bulk commercial shipments.

The 'Technical Premium' UX Philosophy

We built a platform that combines scientific rigour with premium brand experience. Every element — from the scroll-driven fluid simulation to the interactive chromatogram — serves the dual purpose of engaging visitors while demonstrating technical credibility. When complex data is made visible and interactive, it becomes a competitive advantage rather than a usability obstacle.

The heat-reactive ambient interface with scroll-driven fluid simulation and molecular exploration.

Core Interaction Pillars

  • Interactive Chromatogram Engine: Canvas 2D chromatogram with real EMG (Exponentially Modified Gaussian) peak shapes, animated scan lines, and hover tooltips for analytical chemistry transparency.
  • 3D Molecular Explorer: Three.js and React Three Fiber force-directed graph of 47 molecules with IFRA restriction rings and chemical profile inspectors.
  • Global Sourcing Globe: 3D globe with animated arc dashes visualizing supply chain transparency from India to 5 international destination ports.
  • Chemical Signature Search: Reverse search allowing formulation chemists to discover oils by molecular makeup and compliance tolerances.
127 product SKUs with real-time GC/MS peak data, Certificate of Analysis builders, and IFRA compliance overlays.

Education & Gamified Academy

Beyond static spec sheets, the Academy provides certification paths and gamified learning modules covering aromatherapy science, safety standards, and quality assessment, driving the highest conversion signals on the platform.

Overview & Problem: The Tween Dilemma

A "tweenager" is a young individual between 10 to 14 years of age—considered too old to be treated as a young child, yet too young to navigate independent adult life. According to developmental and pediatric clinical research (NCBI & Futurity), tweenage represents a pivotal window where long-term metabolic health risks and dietary behaviors take permanent root.

The Core UX Challenge: Traditional fitness and health apps are designed for adults with heavy data tables, calorie calculations, and clinical terminology that trigger anxiety in young adolescents. Conversely, toddler health games feel patronizing and are swiftly rejected by tweens seeking autonomy.

The Objective: Design a warm, delightful mobile ecosystem that empowers school children to embrace balanced nutrition and physical activity voluntarily—without anxiety, without complex analytics, and with supportive supervision from school teachers and parents.

The Orry ecosystem: single-scroll daily habit dashboard, gamified task validation, and empathetic companion mascot.

Core Design Philosophy: The 3 Pillars

To craft an experience that resonates with 10–14 year-olds while meeting strict pediatric safety standards, we established three non-negotiable architectural pillars:

1. Positive Nutrition & Cultural Relevance

Promote awareness of proper dietary diversity—celebrating local, wholesome ingredients like millets, seasonal fruits, and leafy vegetables with warm regional greetings ("जय जोहार!").

2. Daily Bursts of Joyful Activity

Replace rigid workout regimes with bite-sized daily movement tasks: Do a Suryanamaskar, Run a Mile, or Skip a Rope, integrated directly with reward mechanics.

3. Professional & School Navigation

Transform personal health into a shared, celebrated journey where school teachers assign fun health challenges (Nutrilympics, Meet a Farmer) and celebrate classroom champions.

4. Zero-Anxiety Architecture (No Kids Analytics)

A bold ethical UX decision: No complex analytical charts or calorie obsession on the kid's view. Clinical metrics (BMI, monthly trend lines) remain strictly in the supervisor portal.

Branding, Mascot & Visual Language

Tweens respond to characters that exhibit confidence, playfulness, and warmth. We created Orry—an energetic orange character sporting sunglasses, purple foliage, and sneakers who acts as an enthusiastic companion cheering on daily wins.

Custom iconography system: sport, dumbbell, active movement, nutrition leaf, crown, school, diamond, meal bowl, and fresh produce.

Typography: Poppins — geometric, friendly, and instantly readable on low-cost mobile displays.
Visual Motifs: Soft lavender and mint pastels, rounded cards (36px–40px corner radii), tactile 3D crowns, and glowing diamonds.

The Student Experience: Frictionless Habit Loops

The student application is engineered for maximum simplicity and zero cognitive load:

  • Single-Scroll Simplicity: No convoluted hamburger menus or tab bars on the kid's view. The entire daily routine—remaining hours, morning yoga, hydration, and meal goals—is displayed on one engaging timeline.
  • Gamified Habit Dials: Visual radial progress for hydration and dietary tasks (e.g. Eat 2 Oranges, Drink 12 Glasses) paired with instant Crown and Diamond rewards.
  • Photo & Video Verification ("Record"): Students tap the video record button to capture a 2-second proof of drinking clean water or having their nutritious meal, verifying completion with delight.
Flagship Daily Dashboard: "जय जोहार! Ankita — Tasks of the day" with active countdown, mascot, and calorie balance.
Gamified Habit Tracker: Radial progress wheel, 180 Crowns & 10 Diamonds reward counter, and video camera verification.
Micro-Exercise Tracking: "Run a Mile" with target burn rate and real-time intensity wave graph.
Camera-Verified Task Submission: 2-second playful proof verification for hydration and dietary compliance.

Teacher & School Supervisor Ecosystem

While the student view is gamified and protective, the educator/health supervisor interface provides robust clinical and behavioral monitoring:

  • "Stars of the Day" Classroom Leaderboard: Daily recognition spotlighting students who achieved top consistency in nutrition and physical movement.
  • Student Health Dossier (e.g. Preeti Kumar): Live BMI tracking (19.5 — Just Right), monthly behavioral trend curves, and activity history.
  • Activity Assignment Hub: Teachers assign targeted seasonal and lifestyle modules—including Eat Millets, Nutrilympics, Meet a Farmer, and Hydration Sprints.
  • One-Tap Parent Telephony: Direct phone call and messaging links to parents (Mother & Father) for proactive communication regarding child health milestones.
Classroom Daily Board: "Stars of the Day" leaderboard and searchable student directory with one-tap parental contact.
Student Health Profile: BMI card, activity assignment modules, photo proof review, and monthly performance trajectory.

Overview & Problem

LXRGuide was created to support small businesses across the US in their paid marketing efforts by identifying optimization gaps and suggesting high-impact, actionable recommendations.

The Core Challenge: Before NetElixir hired a dedicated UX team, a beta release of the new platform had been implemented based on blue-sky concepts without user validation. Account managers and SMB owners found the interface overwhelming, difficult to decipher, and disconnected from their daily workflows.

My research focused on:

  • Uncovering specific friction points within the existing AdWords & marketing task journey.
  • Defining measurable task completion metrics to validate redesigned experiences.

Process: Double Diamond & Lean UX

Our process at NetElixir was grounded in the Double Diamond framework and Lean UX iteration, moving through structured phases of Discover, Define, Ideate, and Implement.

The Double Diamond & Lean UX framework applied throughout the LXRGuide redesign lifecycle.

Key Learnings: The 4 Dashboard Principles

A dashboard is an interactive visual interface to present intricate data. Its purpose is to translate complex insights into an easy-to-understand format. An effective design adheres to four essential pillars:

  • 1. Clarity: Promptly and unmistakably conveys intended information.
  • 2. Relevance: Surfaces only metrics that directly tie to operational decisions.
  • 3. Coherence: Maintains structural consistency across all report views.
  • 4. Simplicity (The 5-Second Rule): Users must grasp the purpose and priority within the first 5 seconds of glance, eliminating the need for prolonged study.

Overview & Initial Discovery

LXRSEO is an automated website scanner engineered to measure, benchmark, and improve SEO performance across 62+ parameters.

"When a second is worth more than 60,000 Searches, it's worth stepping back and taking a second look at your Website SEO." — CEO & Co-founder, NetElixir

As UX Designer, I held end-to-end responsibility for the project: stakeholder interviews, domain research, competitive analysis, persona development, open card sorting workshops, wireframing, usability testing, and final visual design.

Domain Research & Competitive Analysis

Using the WordStream Keyword Tool, we researched user search behaviors. Even in low-competition segments, target keywords were searched between 500 and 8,000 times monthly in the US. The highest volume was consistently driven by users seeking simple, accessible SEO diagnostics.

Domain research analyzing keyword volume and intent to frame product scope.

We conducted a rigorous competitive benchmark across 4 leading market tools: SpyFu, Moz, Screaming Frog, and NP Digital. We evaluated features including Keyword Analysis, Site Audits, Backlink Profiling, Custom Reports, and Interface Simplicity.

Competitive feature comparison highlighting the market gap for an intuitive, affordable SMB SEO scanner.

The Opportunity: Most tools were built for enterprise SEO technicians with dense, intimidating interfaces. Content marketers and SMB owners needed a straightforward tool that automated manual audits and provided plain-English guidance.

User Research & Personas

Interviews with technical experts, CTOs, and non-technical business owners revealed several core challenges:

  • Demonstrating SEO value to developers and securing executive buy-in is a constant hurdle.
  • Compared to PPC advertising, SEO lacks immediate, easily trackable feedback loops.
  • Users strongly desired automated scheduled audits and proactive issue alerts.
  • Technical jargon created a steep learning curve that hindered action.
Primary personas: Sarah Bergman (In-house SEO Analyst) & Steven Pope (Freelance Developer).
Storyboard illustrating Sarah's journey from tool fatigue to confident SEO optimization.

Information Architecture & Wireframing

To establish an intuitive structure, I facilitated an Open Card Sorting Workshop with stakeholders and marketers. We mapped out user flows and organized the sitemap into logical modules: Website Landing, Tools, Page Grader, Web Auditor, Competitor Analysis, and Admin.

Sitemap and Information Architecture established through collaborative card sorting.

I began by sketching paper wireframes, then developed digital Figma prototypes emphasizing cognitive load reduction. Complex metrics were segmented into progressive disclosure layers.

Early Figma wireframes focusing on clean data hierarchy, scanability, and actionable items.

Usability Testing & Iterative Refinement

Testing sessions with real users yielded critical design refinements:

  • Comprehensive Overview: The summary screen was redesigned to offer an instant holistic snapshot (Keywords, Backlinks, Speed, and Reach).
  • Intuitive Scoring: Users responded best to a single, authoritative score (0–100) with color-coded status benchmarks.
  • Jargon Translation: Technical terms (e.g. meta descriptions, canonical tags, server response codes) were paired with contextual explanations and one-click fixes.
Usability testing refinements for Link Profiling and Content Relevance scoring.

Final Visual Design

The visual design balances data density with visual breathing room, using clear status indicators, clean typography, and purposeful data visualizations.

The redesigned LXRSEO automated scanner dashboard: authoritative 0-100 Site Health Score, multi-parameter audit categories, and progressive diagnostic disclosure.

Overview & Problem Framing

In India, Astrology (Jyotish) is not horoscopes, entertainment, or fortune-telling. It operates as cultural Decision-Support Software embedded into the daily decisions of 1.4 billion people—spanning marriage alliances, career timing, medical surgeries, property investments, and sacred rituals.

"Market leaders operate on commoditized call-center models ('Talk to Astrologer at ₹120/min'). This creates acute user anxiety, transactional distrust, fear-based upsells, and fragmented experiences. Our mission was to position CosmicTantra as the Vedic Intelligence System of Record." — Product & UX Strategy Framework

The Core Challenge: How do you bridge a 5,000-year-old expert knowledge system with modern digital expectations—making astronomical math accessible to everyday seekers without diluting calculation precision or replacing human scholarly judgement?

Pillar 1: "Shraddha" (Reverence) Meets "Vigyan" (Science)

Users reject spiritual apps that look like generic corporate SaaS dashboards (cold blue grids) or superstitious scam sites (neon yellow promotional banners). The visual design language evokes entering an 18th-century stone Jantar Mantar observatory at dawn.

Subah-e-Banaras (Day Palette)

Warm Sandstone, Ganga Clay, Temple Brass, and Terracotta. Sunlit, high-contrast readability built for Tier 2 and Tier 3 Indian mobile devices.

Kashi Sandhya (Night Palette)

Cosmic Obsidian, Temple Gold, and Diya Amber. Cinematic visual framing honoring the sacred ghats of Varanasi.

Unassailable Mathematical Precision: Calculations are powered by true Sidereal Ephemeris using Lahiri Ayanamsha (24°11'04"), rigorously benchmarked against NASA JPL planetary ephemeris data. The UI declares mathematical conventions openly rather than obscuring calculation nuance.

Core system architecture: Mathematical ephemeris layer, deterministic gateway, and multi-actor consultation workflows.

Pillar 2: Kashi Sahayak V3 — Zero-LLM Conversational Engine

Most conversational AI fails in sacred domains because LLMs hallucinate dates, astrological alignments, and rituals. I architected Kashi Sahayak around a strictly deterministic conversational backbone:

  • Deterministic Pipeline: LanguageNormalizer → WeightedIntentMatcher → EntityExtractor → ConversationState & FlowStack → Deterministic Router → Authored Template Engine.
  • Multi-Intent Interruption Architecture: Real users interrupt guided workflows. If a seeker in the middle of a birth-details intake suddenly asks "Aaj Rahu Kaal kab hai?" (When is Rahu Kaal today?), Kashi calculates the answer from the ephemeris engine, suspends the birth intake into an immutable LIFO FlowStack, and provides an interactive chip to resume without losing state.
  • Indic Token Normalization: Custom boundary handling built specifically for Hindi and Hinglish semantics, avoiding ASCII regex pitfalls across Devanagari Unicode ranges.
Kashi Sahayak conversational interface: Fusing live temple darshan streams with precision astrological intelligence.

Pillar 3: Progressive Kundli & Panchang Experience

We designed the intake and visualization around strict progressive disclosure to prevent overwhelming non-experts:

  • 4-Step Progressive Intake: Name → Date of Birth → Time of Birth (with certainty flags: Exact / Approximate / Unknown) → Strict geocoding resolution (no silent fallback to wrong cities).
  • Consumer First Viewport: An "At a Glance" summary card highlighting Lagna, Moon Rashi, Janma Nakshatra, and Active Mahadasha/Antardasha.
  • Scholarly Workspaces: Deeper tabbed workspaces containing full divisional charts (D1 to D60), Ashtakavarga matrices, and planetary dignity tables for experienced astrologers.
